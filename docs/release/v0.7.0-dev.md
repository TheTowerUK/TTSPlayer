## Comic format (production)

**Supported:** `.cbz` (ZIP-based comic archive)  
**Not supported:** `.cbr` / RAR — convert externally to CBZ and rescan. See [ADR-026 Accepted](../architecture/decisions/ADR-026-reader-surface-architecture.md).

When a legacy CBR item appears in the catalogue, Open Comic is disabled and the app shows:

> CBR archives are not supported. Convert this comic to ZIP/CBZ and rescan the library.

**No UnRAR dependency** in runtime, packaging, or Release builds.
